The house

Room to spread out, water on every side

Tucked into a quiet corner of McHenry, this lakefront cabin gives a big group space to be together and space to get away. The heated outdoor pool and hot tub are the heart of summer here. Just past the pool, a path leads down to a dock on a small private lake where you can fish from the pier or paddle out in the canoe.

Inside, warm cabin decor with a playful bear theme, a full kitchen plus a kitchenette, a separate dining room, an office for anyone who has to log in, and a downstairs bar for evenings in. Every bedroom has easy access to a bathroom, so mornings with twelve people stay calm.

Pistakee Lake and the Chain O'Lakes are a three-minute walk away for boating, and the shops, beaches and restaurants of McHenry and Fox Lake are a short drive.

About the area

Lakes, bogs, classic cars and coasters

McHenry sits on the Fox River at the edge of the Chain O'Lakes, one of the busiest boating areas in the Midwest. Summer means pontoon days, sandbars and fireworks over the water. In the off-season it's nature trails, small-town diners and the antique shops along the river.

3 min walk

Pistakee Lake

Part of the Chain O'Lakes. Rent a boat or jet ski nearby and explore 15 connected lakes.

7 min drive

Volo Bog State Natural Area

A rare quaking bog with a floating boardwalk trail. Great for an easy family walk.

8 min drive

Volo Museum

Hundreds of classic and movie cars, plus antique malls. A good rainy-day pick.

10 min drive

Petersen Park Beach

McHenry's city beach and park on McCullom Lake, with a swimming beach and playgrounds.

8 min drive

Riverside Chocolate Factory

Sweet shop on the river in downtown McHenry, close to restaurants and bars.

25 min drive

Gurnee Mills

One of the Midwest's largest outlet malls, with dining and entertainment.

27 min drive

Six Flags Great America

Roller coasters and the Hurricane Harbor water park for the thrill-seekers.

31 min drive

Lake Geneva, WI

Lakeshore path, mansion boat tours and a lively downtown just over the state line.

FAQ

Questions guests ask

Is the pool heated, and when is it open?

Yes, the outdoor pool is heated. It's a seasonal outdoor pool, generally open late spring through early fall depending on weather. Send us your dates and we'll confirm.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes. Up to two dogs are welcome, each 50 lb or less. Please mention them in your request.

Is the house on the water?

The property has its own access and dock on a small private lake just past the pool, with a canoe and fishing from the pier. The larger Pistakee Lake and the Chain O'Lakes are about a 3-minute walk.

Can we bring or rent a boat?

Several marinas around Pistakee Lake rent pontoons and other boats by the day. Guests regularly rent one for a day on the Chain.

Is it good for families and groups?

That's who it's built for. Four bedrooms, a futon, four and a half baths, two kitchens and a big backyard give a group of up to 12 room to spread out.

The house

Room to spread out, water on every side

Tucked into a quiet corner of McHenry, this lakefront cabin gives a big group space to be together and space to get away. The heated outdoor pool and hot tub are the heart of summer here. Just past the pool, a path leads down to a dock on a small private lake where you can fish from the pier or paddle out in the canoe.

Inside, warm cabin decor with a playful bear theme, a full kitchen plus a kitchenette, a separate dining room, an office for anyone who has to log in, and a downstairs bar for evenings in. Every bedroom has easy access to a bathroom, so mornings with twelve people stay calm.

Pistakee Lake and the Chain O'Lakes are a three-minute walk away for boating, and the shops, beaches and restaurants of McHenry and Fox Lake are a short drive.

Important information

Safety and the water

  • Children must be supervised by an adult at the pool, hot tub, dock and lake at all times. There is no lifeguard.
  • The pool is heated for the summer season. Ask us about exact opening and closing dates for your trip.
  • Canoe use is at your own risk. Please wear life jackets on the water.
  • Boats for Pistakee Lake and the Chain O'Lakes can be rented from local marinas nearby.
  • On-site parking is included. Please don't block neighbors' driveways.
  • The exact address, door code and Wi-Fi details are shared before arrival.

FAQ

Questions guests ask

Is the pool heated, and when is it open?

Yes, the outdoor pool is heated. It's a seasonal outdoor pool, generally open late spring through early fall depending on weather. Send us your dates and we'll confirm.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes. Up to two dogs are welcome, each 50 lb or less. Please mention them in your request.

Is the house on the water?

The property has its own access and dock on a small private lake just past the pool, with a canoe and fishing from the pier. The larger Pistakee Lake and the Chain O'Lakes are about a 3-minute walk.

Can we bring or rent a boat?

Several marinas around Pistakee Lake rent pontoons and other boats by the day. Guests regularly rent one for a day on the Chain.

Is it good for families and groups?

That's who it's built for. Four bedrooms, a futon, four and a half baths, two kitchens and a big backyard give a group of up to 12 room to spread out.

How do I book?

Book instantly on VRBO, or send a request and we'll reply with a quote and any questions.

Is there somewhere to work?

Yes, there's a dedicated office space in the house.

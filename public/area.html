About the area

Lakes, bogs, classic cars and coasters

McHenry sits on the Fox River at the edge of the Chain O'Lakes, one of the busiest boating areas in the Midwest. Summer means pontoon days, sandbars and fireworks over the water. In the off-season it's nature trails, small-town diners and the antique shops along the river.

3 min walk

Pistakee Lake

Part of the Chain O'Lakes. Rent a boat or jet ski nearby and explore 15 connected lakes.

7 min drive

Volo Bog State Natural Area

A rare quaking bog with a floating boardwalk trail. Great for an easy family walk.

8 min drive

Volo Museum

Hundreds of classic and movie cars, plus antique malls. A good rainy-day pick.

10 min drive

Petersen Park Beach

McHenry's city beach and park on McCullom Lake, with a swimming beach and playgrounds.

8 min drive

Riverside Chocolate Factory

Sweet shop on the river in downtown McHenry, close to restaurants and bars.

25 min drive

Gurnee Mills

One of the Midwest's largest outlet malls, with dining and entertainment.

27 min drive

Six Flags Great America

Roller coasters and the Hurricane Harbor water park for the thrill-seekers.

31 min drive

Lake Geneva, WI

Lakeshore path, mansion boat tours and a lively downtown just over the state line.
